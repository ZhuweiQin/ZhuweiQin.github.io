I am an **Associate Professor** of Computer Engineering in the School of Engineering, College of Science & Engineering at **San Francisco State University (SFSU)**, where I direct the [Mobile and Intelligent Computing (MIC) Laboratory](http://sfsu-miclab.org/). I am also an Adjunct Faculty member in Electrical and Computer Engineering at George Mason University, teaching graduate courses on mobile systems and distributed & federated learning.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from George Mason University in 2020, my M.S. from Oregon State University, and my B.E. in Automation from Tianjin University of Science & Technology.

My research makes AI **efficient, adaptive, and deployable** on resource-constrained devices, and applies it to human-machine interfaces and engineering education. My work has appeared in venues such as IEEE TCAD, KDD, IJCAI, ICCAD, DATE and BMVC (38 publications, 900+ citations), and is supported by the NSF, the Department of Defense, Sony, and SFSU.

Research Interests
======
- **Efficient AI Computing** — edge & mobile model acceleration, LLM compression and domain adaptation, federated & decentralized learning
- **Intelligent Sensing & Neural-Machine Interfaces** — high-density EMG gesture recognition, on-device learning for rehabilitation and prosthetics, wearable biomedical sensing
- **AI for Education** — human-centered and trustworthy AI systems, intelligent tutoring, support for neurodiverse engineering students
