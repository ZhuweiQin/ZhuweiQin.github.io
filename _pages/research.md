My group, the [Mobile and Intelligent Computing (MIC) Laboratory](http://sfsu-miclab.org/), works on three connected directions.

## Efficient AI Computing

Deep models are increasingly expected to run where the data is — on phones, microcontrollers and wearables — under tight compute, memory and energy budgets. We develop techniques that make models smaller, faster and adaptive:

- **Model compression and reconfiguration** — interpretability-guided filter pruning and class-adaptive CNN reconfiguration for mobile systems (CAPTOR/CAPTORX, TCAD; Functionality-Oriented Pruning, BMVC; VoCaM, ICCAD).
- **LLM compression and domain adaptation** — propagation-aware pruning that adapts large language models to new domains at low cost (EfficientXpert, NeurIPS Workshop 2026).
- **Federated and decentralized learning** — feature-aligned aggregation for heterogeneous clients (Fed2, KDD) and collaborative on-device training at the edge.

## Intelligent Sensing & Neural-Machine Interfaces

We build real-time systems that read neuromuscular signals and run learning on the device itself:

- High-density EMG gesture recognition with robust deep models (GAN-augmented HD-EMG, NER; NeuroEdge, BioCAS 2026).
- On-device learning and adaptation for myoelectric interfaces (mMyoHMI, BioRob; EdgeEMG, BSN).
- Neural-controlled bionic arms and rehabilitation exoskeletons on low-power microcontrollers, in collaboration with **Sony** (Spresense).

## AI for Education

We design human-centered AI systems that support how engineering students learn and work together:

- The AI Collaborative (AICO) and SCAPE platforms for personalized engineering learning.
- AI-driven support for neurodiverse students in engineering teamwork (NSF).
- Responsible-AI perspectives of engineering students at a Hispanic-Serving Institution.
